About Liftwise

Many beginners wander the gym without a plan. Liftwise gives you a split, a log, and a timer in one place, with no account and no server.

Beginner tips

How do I choose a split?

Push, pull, and legs train different muscles, so one rests while another works. Rotate through them, and use Full body when your week is short.

How many sets and reps?

Start with 3 sets of 8 to 12 reps. Choose a weight that makes the last two reps hard but clean.

How long should I rest?

60 seconds for light isolation work, 90 for most exercises, and 120 for heavy lifts like squats and presses.

When should I add weight?

When every set hits the top of your rep range with good form, add a small amount next session.
